Reading the map

Each hexagon counts outdoor shoot records. Coral marks predominantly feature films, violet marks television and web productions, and rose blends the two. A deeper tint means more records in that area.

Choose a year, tap a coloured area for its titles and addresses, or use the magnifier to follow a production across Paris. A title search brightens its locations so that even a single shoot is visible.

The map includes geocoded records with Paris postcodes, from 2016 through 2025. Titles and addresses follow the archive. Multiple records may refer to the same production or street.
